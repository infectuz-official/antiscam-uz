Antiscam.uz

Antiscam.uz is an independent educational initiative focused on scam prevention, digital literacy, and online safety.

About the Project

The project aims to raise awareness about online fraud, malicious applications, financial scams, and common digital threats. Antiscam.uz provides educational materials and analytical content designed to help users recognize and avoid scams.

Founder & Ownership

Antiscam.uz was founded in 2025 by infectuz as an independent initiative. The project is privately owned and operated and is not affiliated with any governmental or commercial organization.

The project was conceived, initiated, and independently developed by infectuz. No partnerships, shared ownership, or co-building agreements exist.
